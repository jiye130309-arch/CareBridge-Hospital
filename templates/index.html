About CareBridge

Hospital care designed around the patient journey.

CareBridge Hospital brings emergency care, specialist clinics, diagnostics and pharmacy services together in one clear digital home. The goal of this website is to make it easy to understand what the hospital offers and how to book a visit.

This homepage is the first step of the web migration. Later modules will add more pages, data storage and deployment.

Emergency Care

General Medicine

Pediatrics
